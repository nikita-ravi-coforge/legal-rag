## Exercise Good Judgment

In some situations, it can be challenging to know the right action to take. If you are unsure what to do, follow the steps in the decision tree below.

Step 1: Gather Information. What are the facts? Who is involved or affected (patients, healthcare providers, regulators, company)? What policies, codes, regulations, or laws apply? If information is missing, seek guidance from Ethics & Compliance and/or Legal. If it is unclear whether or how laws or regulations would apply, seek guidance from Legal. If it is unclear whether or how code provisions would apply, seek guidance from Ethics & Compliance and Legal. If information is complete, proceed to Step 2.

Step 2: Evaluate the Options. Is it honest and transparent? Is it consistent with Lilly values? Is it compliant with company policies and industry codes? If any answer is NO or UNCERTAIN, seek guidance from Ethics & Compliance and/or Legal. If all answers are YES, proceed to Step 3.

Step 3: Consider the Impact. Could this decision harm patients, public trust, or the company's reputation? Are there unintended consequences? Would I be comfortable if this appeared in the news? If there is potential harm, reassess or seek guidance from Ethics & Compliance and/or Legal. If no harm is identified, proceed to Step 4.

Step 4: Make the Decision. Choose the most ethical and compliant option. Document your rationale and actions, as appropriate. Inform relevant stakeholders.

Step 5: Reflect and Learn. What went well? What could be improved? Are there lessons to share with your team?
