# Red Book: Code of Business Conduct

## Who We Are

For 150 years, we’ve been turning science into medicine, taking on the toughest diseases to help improve lives in ways that have changed the course of human health. Today, we continue this pursuit in disease areas like Alzheimer’s, cancer, diabetes, immunology, and obesity to help as many people as possible live healthier lives. Helping people stay healthy takes determination, courage, and innovation. There are no simple solutions, but we persevere because we believe everyone deserves the chance to live a healthy life.

## Our Purpose

Lilly unites caring with discovery to create medicines that make life better for people around the world.

## Our Values

Integrity, Excellence, and Respect for People.

## About the Red Book

Our Lilly values inspire us to do the right things for the right reasons. The Red Book sets the expectation for behavior that we demonstrate every day. We are committed to act legally and ethically, following both the letter and the spirit of the laws, regulations, policies, and procedures that govern our business, and we expect our third parties, vendors, and partners to do the same. We understand our responsibilities to Lilly, to each other, and to those we serve. We apply good judgment, and we seek help when we’re not sure of the right thing to do. We never underestimate the critical role each of us plays in making life better for people around the world.

## Quote from Our CEO

“Trust is the foundation of Lilly’s purpose, strategy, and global operations. Every day we must earn that trust through our actions and words—from patients, partners, and communities worldwide. The Red Book provides each of us with a framework for sound decisions and ethical actions; it is core to being a part of TEAM Lilly. Together, we commit to maintaining the highest standards in our products, our conduct, and our business practices everywhere we operate. Thank you for upholding these principles in all you do.”

Dave Ricks, Chief Executive Officer

## Our Responsibilities: Respecting People

We value the diversity of backgrounds, experiences, skills, and perspectives of everyone around the world. We are better when we include everyone. We are committed to creating an accessible environment by striving to remove barriers for people of all abilities who work at Lilly, do business with us, and use our products. We adhere to fair employment practices and do not tolerate inappropriate conduct, including acts of discrimination, retaliation, or harassment.

## Our Responsibilities: Protecting People, the Environment, and Our Assets

We believe in the inherent equality of all people, regardless of where they were born, where they live, ethnicity, race, gender, sexual orientation, or disability. We endeavor to maintain a secure workplace and protect people and the communities in which we operate and serve. We are focused on continuously improving our health and safety practices to promote the well-being of our people. We are committed to conducting business in a responsible and environmentally sustainable manner. We dedicate ourselves to a robust security culture to protect our people and brand from harm, and our assets from loss, theft, or damage. Each of us is responsible for implementing our security practices and applying them in our daily activities.

## Our Responsibilities: Conducting Research and Development

We conduct research and development activities consistent with bioethics principles and sound scientific methodologies. We disclose the results of our research in an accurate, objective, balanced, and timely manner.

## Our Responsibilities: Assuring Quality

We work to assure a consistent supply of quality products and provide complete and accurate information on their safety and effectiveness. We research, develop, manufacture, and supply products and information using appropriate science, facilities, and materials, in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

## Our Responsibilities: Preventing Corruption

We recognize that bribery, fraud, and other acts of dishonesty are a betrayal of trust. We don’t offer, provide, authorize, or accept anything of value to inappropriately influence a decision or gain an unfair advantage. Our commitment to operating with high ethical standards extends to all business relationships and activities around the world.

## Our Responsibilities: Communicating Honestly

We disclose only the information we are authorized to share and do so in an honest, transparent, and accurate manner. When external inquiries arise, we refer them to the appropriate internal experts and ensure all necessary approvals are obtained based on the content and audience.

## Our Responsibilities: Marketing Responsibly

We promote our medications in compliance with applicable laws, regulations, and industry codes. We believe that healthcare providers deserve clear, reliable, and scientifically validated information to make the best decisions for their patients. Every marketing communication we share is grounded in evidence, aligned with approved labeling, and presented with fairness and balance.

## Our Responsibilities: Managing and Protecting Information

We treat confidential information as a valuable asset and ensure it is protected, accurate, and available to those who need it. Our information-management practices reflect the sensitivity and purpose of the data, and we use the right tools and methods to handle it responsibly. We respect, and do not knowingly violate, the legitimate information rights of others. Across all our global operations, we work to prevent unauthorized access, unintentional disclosure, compromise, theft, destruction, and disruptions to access of information and manage it carefully throughout its entire life cycle.

## Our Responsibilities: Respecting Personal Information and Privacy

We are committed to the ethical management of all personal information. We’re open and honest about how we collect, manage, use, and disclose personal information, and we’re intentional about protecting it. We strive to only share personal information with those who are authorized and have a legitimate business need to know.

## Our Responsibilities: Using Artificial Intelligence Responsibly

We are committed to the responsible use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and hold ourselves accountable through robust governance, clear lines of responsibility, monitoring, and improvement. We seek to protect against unintended applications, unintended access, and harm associated with use of AI and strive to mitigate any inherent bias or discrimination in data, information, or algorithms. We employ technology that produces valid and reliable outputs and promote transparency in the use of AI.

## Our Responsibilities: Maintaining Financial Integrity

We obtain all necessary approvals before committing funds on behalf of Lilly, and we follow all internal processes, controls, and accounting principles. We ensure that our records accurately, fairly, and completely reflect all transactions.

## Our Responsibilities: Speaking Up

We speak up when we experience, observe, or reasonably suspect something that could harm Lilly or those we serve. Concerns should be reported out of respect for one another, our business partners, and those we serve. We recognize that speaking up—even if anonymously— is our right and our responsibility, and that taking no action when action is warranted can have serious consequences. We share concerns openly and honestly, knowing that Lilly will not tolerate acts of retaliation.

## Exercise Good Judgment

In some situations, it can be challenging to know the right action to take. If you are unsure what to do, follow the steps in the decision tree below.

Step 1: Gather Information. What are the facts? Who is involved or affected (patients, healthcare providers, regulators, company)? What policies, codes, regulations, or laws apply? If information is missing, seek guidance from Ethics & Compliance and/or Legal. If it is unclear whether or how laws or regulations would apply, seek guidance from Legal. If it is unclear whether or how code provisions would apply, seek guidance from Ethics & Compliance and Legal. If information is complete, proceed to Step 2.

Step 2: Evaluate the Options. Is it honest and transparent? Is it consistent with Lilly values? Is it compliant with company policies and industry codes? If any answer is NO or UNCERTAIN, seek guidance from Ethics & Compliance and/or Legal. If all answers are YES, proceed to Step 3.

Step 3: Consider the Impact. Could this decision harm patients, public trust, or the company's reputation? Are there unintended consequences? Would I be comfortable if this appeared in the news? If there is potential harm, reassess or seek guidance from Ethics & Compliance and/or Legal. If no harm is identified, proceed to Step 4.

Step 4: Make the Decision. Choose the most ethical and compliant option. Document your rationale and actions, as appropriate. Inform relevant stakeholders.

Step 5: Reflect and Learn. What went well? What could be improved? Are there lessons to share with your team?

## Speaking Up: How to Report a Concern

We ask anyone, whether working for Lilly or not, to speak up with concerns about possible violations of laws and regulations, the Red Book, or company policies or procedures. Concerns can be submitted at https://speakup.lilly.com.

## Speaking Up: Investigations Overview

When a concern is raised, we take it seriously and follow up to understand what happened. We will review the concern, investigate, as appropriate, and consult the Red Book and Lilly policies and procedures. We may use investigation findings to clarify processes or procedures, provide feedback or coaching, or to implement other corrective measures, including formal discipline, when appropriate. Investigation details, including discipline, are treated as confidential.
